IPAP Base Study Program

400 questions from your four challenging slide-only quiz packs. Adaptive repetition, an incorrect bank, instant feedback, source-slide notes, and targeted slide-review recommendations, manual Incorrect Bank removal, and previous/next question navigation, bad-question removal before or after answering, an unasked-this-cycle study mode, and portable cross-device progress transfer are built in.

Adaptive mastery: a missed question enters the Incorrect Bank. You can manually remove it at any time, or let it stay there until you answer it correctly 3 times in a row. Adaptive quizzes deliberately pull bank questions more often until they are mastered.

Coverage rule: questions you answer correctly and that are not in the active Incorrect Bank will not recycle until every question in your selected test pool has been shown once. The only exception is an active Incorrect Bank question, which may repeat sooner so you can reach your selected mastery streak.
